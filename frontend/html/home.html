What can it do?

The pictures below are real outputs from our own code, not mock-ups.

Text to Image

Write a prompt, pick the Realistic or Anime model, and Stable Diffusion Forge on PC2 paints it for you.

a cozy bookshop at night, anime style Anime

sent to Forge: /sdapi/v1/txt2img

Background Removal

Upload a photo and rembg cuts out the subject. You get a transparent PNG.

transparent PNG

Histogram Matching

Borrow the colors of one photo and pour them into another. We match the histogram of each RGB channel through its CDF.

+ =

real output from hismat.py

Gaussian Blur

Soften a photo with low, medium or high strength. Drag the line to compare.

before after

Grayscale & Contrast

Turn a photo gray, then stretch its contrast with histogram equalization.

How it works

Four computers on one network, each with its own job.

you open the web app here!

handles requests, jobs and logins

Browser

  • The user opens LUMA

PC1: Frontend + Nginx

  • Serves the web pages
  • Reverse proxy for /api/*

PC3: Flask Backend

  • APIs and login
  • Blur, histogram matching, contrast
  • Talks to the AI and the database

PC2: Forge AI

  • Text to image
  • Background removal (rembg)

PC4: PostgreSQL

  • Users
  • Image job logs
